fullset

Your retro game collection, and a diary for every game.

fullset keeps track of the games you own — shelf by shelf, console by console — and gives each one a place to write down what playing it was like. Notes, photos, voice memos, and the date you first played it.

Everything stays on your phone. No account, no server, nothing to sign up for.

What it does

Shelves

Your physical collection grouped by console, with real box art and how complete each copy is — loose, boxed, or complete in box.

A diary per game

Dated notes, photos, and voice memos. Voice is transcribed on the device itself.

Offline catalogs

26,884 games across 37 lists, bundled with the app. Search works with no connection at all.

Region aware

The same game is a different product in each market — its own title, date, and catalog number. fullset keeps them apart.

Consoles

NES · Super Nintendo · Nintendo 64 · GameCube · PlayStation · PlayStation 2 · PlayStation 3 · PlayStation 5 · SG-1000 · Master System · Genesis / Mega Drive · Game Gear · Sega CD · 32X · Saturn · Dreamcast

Each one ships with separate lists for North America, Japan and Europe, so your Japanese import shows the box, the release date and the catalog number that actually match the copy on your shelf.

Just a diary?

If you don't want to track a collection, the first screen lets you say so and the shelves stay out of your way. You can change your mind later — nothing is deleted either way.

Your data is yours

  • Stored locally, in a database on your device.
  • Voice notes are transcribed on the phone. Audio never leaves it.
  • Export everything as JSON, or as a ZIP with your photos included.
  • The only network calls are for cover images.

Get it

fullset is free and open source under the MIT license. It runs on Android today; an iOS version is in progress.
